The Venue

Nestled at the foothills of the Bukhansan mountains, Sunwoongak is a stunning traditional Korean estate, known as a hanok. With its sweeping wooden rooflines and serene natural surroundings, it offers a peaceful retreat from the bustling city of Seoul. We are thrilled to share this beautiful piece of Korean heritage with you as we celebrate our marriage.

Weather & Dress Code

Early May is one of the most beautiful times to visit South Korea. You can expect mild, pleasant spring weather, with daytime temperatures generally hovering around 18–22°C. Evenings cool down to around 10–13°C, so a light jacket or wrap is recommended for the afterparty. Historically, early May offers clear, sunny skies with very little chance of rain.

For the celebration, we kindly request formal or semi-formal attire—suits for gentlemen and elegant dresses or equivalent for ladies. Please note that the traditional hanok venue features natural stone and wooden pathways, so we highly recommend choosing comfortable footwear for the day.

Travel & Logistics

Where to Stay

For our guests traveling from Finland, we highly recommend booking your hotel in the Myeongdong area. It is centrally located and serves as the perfect base for exploring the city. Myeongdong is famous for its vibrant street food night markets, fantastic shopping, and lively atmosphere, while also offering seamless public transport connections to both historical palaces and modern districts.

Getting to the Venue

Our venue, Sunwoongak, is tucked away in the beautiful nature of northern Seoul. The easiest way to arrive via public transit is to take the subway to Bukhansan Ui station. From there, a dedicated shuttle bus will be operating to bring guests directly to the venue doors.

For those arriving by car, there is a large, spacious parking area near the venue. A continuous shuttle service is provided from the parking lot directly to the estate.

The Day's Flow

Korean weddings have a slightly different rhythm than Finnish ones! The main event is beautifully concise, focusing on the ceremony and a shared meal in the early afternoon.
