Welcome

We are overjoyed to welcome you to South Korea to celebrate our marriage. On the 1st of May, 2027, we will gather with our closest family and friends to exchange our vows and begin our next chapter together. Bringing our two cultures and families together in Seoul is a dream come true, and having you there to share in this special day would mean the world to us.

The Venue

Nestled at the foothills of the Bukhansan mountains, Sunwoongak is a stunning traditional Korean estate, known as a hanok. With its sweeping wooden rooflines and serene natural surroundings, it offers a peaceful retreat from the bustling city of Seoul. We are thrilled to share this beautiful piece of Korean heritage with you as we celebrate our marriage.

Travel & Logistics

Where to Stay

For our guests traveling from Finland, we highly recommend booking your hotel in the Myeongdong area. It is centrally located, packed with great food, and offers excellent public transport connections to explore the rest of Seoul.

Getting to the Venue

Our venue, Sunwoongak, is tucked away in the beautiful nature of northern Seoul. The easiest way to arrive is to take the subway to Bukhansan Ui station. From there, a dedicated shuttle bus will be operating to bring guests directly to the venue doors.

The Day's Flow

Korean weddings have a slightly different rhythm than Finnish ones! The main event is beautifully concise, focusing on the ceremony and a shared meal in the early afternoon.

  • 11:00 Guests arrive. Welcome drinks and mingling.
  • 12:00 The wedding ceremony begins.
  • 12:30 Dining buffet opens. Relaxing and hanging out at the venue.
  • 15:00 Getting back to Seoul, time for a short break.
  • 18:00 Afterparty in Seoul.
  • 00:00 Nightlife up to everyone's consideration.

The Afterparty

For our younger guests, the celebration continues in the evening. We will be hosting an afterparty starting around 6:00 PM somewhere in central Seoul. The exact venue is currently being finalized, and we will update this page as soon as it is booked.
